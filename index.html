About the Z Fighters

The Z Fighters are Earth’s elite defenders against the universe’s most powerful threats. Their story blends relentless training, strategic teamwork, and legendary transformations.

Primary Characters

Goku, Vegeta, Gohan, Piccolo, and Krillin form the core group that protects Earth through every peril—from Frieza’s tyranny to Majin Buu’s chaos.

Professional Design

Modern gradients, 3D motion, and clean navigation create an immersive experience while keeping the narrative accessible for fans and newcomers.

Core Heroes

Meet the Warriors

Son Goku

The main protagonist and Saiyan warrior raised on Earth. Goku constantly pushes beyond his limits and taps into divine forms like Ultra Instinct.

Vegeta

The proud Saiyan prince who evolves from ruthless rival to devoted family man. Vegeta’s discipline and rivalry with Goku define his growth.

Son Gohan

Goku’s eldest son with immense hidden potential. Gohan combines intellect with explosive strength when protecting the world.

Piccolo

A brilliant Namekian warrior who transformed from conqueror to mentor. Piccolo is a tactical leader and a disciplined teacher to Gohan.

Krillin

Earth’s bravest human fighter. Krillin’s courage and resilience make him a vital ally, even against foes who dwarf his raw power.

Universe Threats

Iconic Villains

Frieza

The ruthless space tyrant who destroyed the Saiyan homeworld and is Goku’s definitive arch-nemesis.

Cell

A bio-android built from the universe’s greatest fighters. Cell seeks perfection by absorbing rivals and testing his limits.

Majin Buu

A chaotic magical entity with shape-shifting power. His unpredictable threat forces the Z Fighters to work together like never before.
